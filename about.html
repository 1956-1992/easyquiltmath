Every quilter knows the moment: you are standing in the fabric store, quilt top finished, trying to do seam math in your head while the cutting counter waits.

Easy Quilt Math exists for that moment. Type in your quilt’s size and it tells you — in plain fractions, the way fabric is actually cut — how much backing, binding, and batting to buy. No decimals like “3.222 yards.” No sign-up wall. No tiny print.

The site is built senior-first on purpose: big type, big buttons, one screen, answers you can print and carry to the store. It is free, and it runs entirely in your browser — the numbers you type never leave your device.

Easy Quilt Math is a small independent project, made with care for the quilting community. If it saved you a second trip to the fabric store, it did its job.
